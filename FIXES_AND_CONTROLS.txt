TITANS-63 Adventure - fixed project

OPEN AND RUN
1. Extract the whole ZIP into a new folder.
2. In Godot 4, import/open project.godot from the extracted folder.
3. Allow the model assets to import, then press F6 for mars.tscn or F5.

CONTROLS
W: forward (-Z), S: backward (+Z), A: left (-X), D: right (+X).
Directions are relative to the fixed follow camera/world axes.
Mouse: look around. W/S move forward/backward relative to your view.
A/D strafe left/right relative to your view.
Escape: release the mouse. Left click: capture it again.
Moon_Walk plays while moving and pauses when movement stops.
Speed and moon_gravity can be adjusted on the asronat node.
Camera Motion Strength controls the floating head movement; set it to 0
for a steady view. Default 0.35 keeps movement gentle.
Camera Smoothing controls how quickly the camera follows and settles.
Mouse Sensitivity controls look speed.

FIXES
- Replaced the placeholder StaticBody3D script with a CharacterBody3D controller.
- Changed the asronat node to CharacterBody3D and attached its script.
- Connected the imported AnimationPlayer automatically; enabled Moon_Walk looping.
- Kept W's existing front action and added S/back, A/left, D/right.
- Matched the floor collision to its 30 x 0.2 x 30 visual mesh.
- Positioned the floor top at zero and aligned the player capsule above it.
- Moved the camera to a first-person eye pivot at 1.65m.
- Added mouse look with a vertical angle limit and camera-relative WASD.
- Added smoothed head-animation bob and subtle movement tilt.
- Filtered slow animation drift and capped camera offsets.
- Hid the local model from the first-person camera to avoid helmet clipping.
- Turned the visual model to face W and added a directional light.
- Set the main scene to its file path so a fresh import works without a UID cache.
- Removed a mislabeled .tres that contained binary GLB data. Its byte-identical
  correctly named GLB remains in the project, so no unique model data was lost.

VALIDATION
Tested using Godot 4.6.3 headless: scene loading, floor collision, all four
movement directions, normalized diagonal speed, stationary idle, and animation
play/pause. Also tested eye height, head-bone tracking, bounded bob, idle
settling, turned movement, mouse look, and cursor release.
Interactive graphics were not visually tested.

Optional command-line check from this folder:
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . --script res://tests/movement_smoke_test.gd

All model and texture source assets are included.
